## Make route features and hooks easier to use

Upload is **opt-in**, same as download, delete, and multipart. Omitting
`upload` no longer enables an unconstrained presign endpoint — set
`upload: true` or an options object.

`upload.multipart.sessionGuard` is now `upload.multipart.guard`. Complete
still runs that hook (`action: "complete"`) then `confirmGuard`.

`useDelete` gains `remove(key)` for a one-shot delete without the confirm
step. `chainHooks` exports from `@dimah-s3/server`. `S3UploadError` and
`fileNameFromKey` are public.

`purgeStalePendingObjects` and `createObjectAccessGuard` take `client`
(same name as `db({ client })`). `getFileExtension` and
`mapStorageObjectRow` are no longer public.
